# Pulp Lab

A research and ideation site about plastic-free packaging, built as a thesis prototype on helping graphic designers adopt more eco-friendly practices.

Open `pulp-lab.html` in a browser (or serve the folder, e.g. `python3 -m http.server`).

## Pages
- **Plastic**: everyday items that are still plastic, with recycling data and eco swaps (hover to fan them out).
- **Swaps**: products already on shelves plastic-free, with before/after and costs.
- **Ideate**: a drawing studio that suggests materials (uses Claude when run inside the Claude artifact viewer; falls back to the built-in library).
- **Material Lab**: material close-ups, specs, compliance flags and a UK/US/EU packaging tax and fee calculator.
- **Real World**: 50 brand case studies with sourced figures.
- **Rankings**: company plastic packaging footprints.

## Data
- `data-cases.js`, `data-companies.js`, `data-buy.js`, `data-prices.js`, `data-specs.js`: compiled November 2026; sources are linked in the page.

## Image and footage rights
- `img/brand/` holds the brands' own product photos, used for study only. Do not publish them without permission.
- Other product and material photos are AI-generated (FLORA).
- `video/` clips are free Pexels footage (creators credited on the page); the video home page is currently switched off (`SHOW_HOME`).
